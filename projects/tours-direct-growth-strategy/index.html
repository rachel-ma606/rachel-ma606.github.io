Tours Direct Digital Performance & Market Growth Strategy

A real-client marketing analytics and strategy project combining market research, competitor analysis, CRM and campaign data, churn prediction, Power BI reporting, and later website migration implementation.

View Evidence Visit Tours Direct website →

Project Overview

Tours Direct is a New Zealand-owned escorted travel company specialising in culturally immersive group tours for mature travellers. The company faced a post-COVID growth challenge involving industry contraction, increased competition, an ageing customer base, reduced database size, and declining marketing efficiency.

The industry project was designed to provide an evidence-based roadmap for strategic renewal. The analysis combined national travel statistics, Salesforce data, Mailchimp campaign and unsubscribe data, competitor research, and travel trend datasets to evaluate market opportunities, competitive positioning, marketing effectiveness, churn risk, and potential younger customer segments.

Company Context

Tours Direct focuses on escorted, small-group international tours with curated itineraries, experienced tour leaders, and culturally rich destinations. The company website is linked here for public context.

Consulting & Analytics Scope

The project moved from business problem framing to data preparation, exploratory analysis, predictive modelling, and strategy recommendations.

Market Opportunity Analysis

Analysed New Zealand outbound travel patterns, age segments, seasonality, and destination growth to identify where Tours Direct could prioritise future market opportunities.

Competitive Positioning

Benchmarked 12 escorted travel operators using tour volume and pricing tier, then applied positioning and SWOT analysis to clarify Tours Direct’s differentiation.

Marketing & Customer Analytics

Analysed Salesforce transactions, Mailchimp campaign data, lead sources, booking behaviour, retention patterns, and unsubscribe reasons to understand engagement quality.

Churn Prediction & Retention Strategy

Built a Random Forest churn model using merged Salesforce and Mailchimp data to identify at-risk customers and support targeted reactivation planning.

Lead Source Effectiveness

Compared lead generation and confirmed bookings to identify a gap between digital visibility and conversion quality.

Churn Model Performance

Evaluated model performance using ROC-AUC, recall, precision, and F1-score to support customer churn prediction and retention planning.

Customer Risk Segmentation

Translated model probabilities into low, medium, and high churn-risk groups to support targeted retention actions.

Key Outcomes

Identified market and destination opportunities

The 40–69 age group represented the largest outbound travel segment, while destinations such as China, Japan, Central Asia, and Southeast Asia showed strong growth signals.

Clarified competitive positioning

Tours Direct occupied a premium, lower-volume, culturally immersive niche, differentiated by complex itinerary planning, Kiwi tour leaders, and inclusive group travel.

Found digital conversion gaps

Traditional channels generated stronger confirmed bookings, while digital channels created visibility but weaker conversion, highlighting the need for better digital lead nurturing.

Built churn-based retention logic

The churn model helped identify disengagement risk patterns, supporting more targeted retention, reactivation, and lifecycle marketing recommendations.
